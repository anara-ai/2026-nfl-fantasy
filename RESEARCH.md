## What the board measures

| Field | Meaning | Limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Overall / positional rank | FantasyPros half-PPR Expert Consensus Ranking | Closest public format, not bespoke projections for every scoring setting |
| Tier | Supplied FantasyPros overall tier | A broad consensus grouping, not a positional scarcity model |
| Expert range / SD | Source-reported best, worst and standard deviation of expert ranks | Not a points range, probability, injury estimate or guarantee of availability |

| Yahoo ADP | Decimal **All Drafts** average pick | Public standard-settings pool, not a ten-team-only or last-seven-days sample |
| Yahoo gap | ADP minus ECR | Positive means later market selection; eight picks triggers a display badge, not statistical significance |
| Status | Yahoo designation captured September 5 | Q is not automatically the final Week 1 game designation |
| Read / Plan | Our draft judgment | Conditional guidance, not another supposedly independent ranking source |

## Pick-3 strategy

Nine starters plus five bench positions mean **14 rounds**, not 15. Your sequence:
**3, 18, 23, 38, 43, 58, 63, 78, 83, 98, 103, 118, 123, 138.**
There are four opponent selections between 18 and 23, then fourteen between 23
and 38. Treat each close pair as one decision: which tier is likely to disappear?
Keep at least three alternatives rather than interpreting ADP as a probability.

| Turn | Main decision | Contingency |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Gibbs/Bijan if available, otherwise Chase | Puka/JSN if material new evidence changes the top tier |
| 18 | Walker/Achane if they fall; Hampton is a practical RB anchor | London, Nico, A.J. Brown or Bowers if they offer the stronger tier |
| 23 | After Chase + RB: another top WR or Bowers/McBride | After Chase + WR/TE: assess the remaining RB tier without forcing a reach |
| 38 / 43 | Rice/Flowers/Waddle/Ladd/Garrett, RB need, or Loveland | Lamar at 38 / Maye at 43 only if their advantage justifies the cost |
| 58 / 63 | Burden/Jameson, Tuten/Price, or a falling QB | Hurts/Daniels/Burrow at 58; Caleb at 63; health checks for uncertain players |
| 78 / 83 | Resolve QB/TE; compare Lloyd and the WR depth | Herbert/Dak/Lawrence may go earlier; do not assume cheap QB availability |
| 98 / 103 | Sutton/Quentin Johnston, RB upside, late QB/TE if needed | Purdy/Nix, Kincaid or other remaining starters |
| 118 | Final skill pick with a credible path to useful volume | Coleman/Mason; Juwan or Goff if a starting slot remains open |
| 123 / 138 | One defense and one kicker | Prefer an opening matchup over a season-long defense brand |

These are target windows, not predicted selections. The app's Plan tab contains
the detailed branches and tappable targets.

Three plausible openings to rehearse:

- **Chase → Hampton → A.J. Brown:** balanced RB/WR start; compare TE versus RB depth
  at 38/43. If Brown is gone, compare Nico, Pickens, Olave and the elite TEs.
- **Chase → London → Bowers:** strong receiving/TE start; seek an RB at 38/43 and
  prioritize additional active backs later. This works only if you accept the
  resulting RB uncertainty; it is not a mandate to wait on RB.
- **Gibbs/Bijan → London/Brown → another strong WR or TE:** an unexpected elite RB
  fall changes needs. Do not force another early RB just because the original
  plan began with Chase.

One QB and one TE are the default because the league starts one of each. The
preferred final construction is **1 QB, 5 RB, 5 WR, 1 TE, 1 K, 1 DEF**; 4 RB/6 WR
also works. A second QB/TE needs a specific health or streaming purpose. Avoid
buying several injured starters plus multiple stashes on a five-player bench.
Drafting someone else's contingent RB can be more useful than automatically
buying all your own backups.

## Preparation and draft-night routine

1. Confirm draft timing, snake/no-keeper assumptions and whether the actual Yahoo
   setup changes draft-to-IR behavior. Two IR positions do not automatically add
   draft selections. Check eligibility on the player in your league.
   [Yahoo IR help](https://help.yahoo.com/kb/SLN28136.html)
2. Run at least three slot-3 mocks covering the openings above. Include one early
   TE path, one late TE path, and a draft where QBs go aggressively. Note targets
   that fail to reach your turns instead of inventing reach probabilities.
3. Before each pick, identify remaining tier, roster need and three alternatives.
   A positional run can leave value elsewhere; do not chase simply to feel caught up.
4. Before the draft, reopen the phone app online, check the visible research date,
   and try Draft → Undo. Picks are stored on that device. Offline mode uses the
   last cached research, and external source links require connectivity.
5. Refresh official practice reports, roster moves and game timing near the actual
   draft. Review the highest-risk starters first, then the last skill pick and
   opening defense. This September 5 snapshot is not self-updating.
6. Because benched players are not locked, post-draft bench management may provide
   flexibility, subject to the league's waiver rules. Check the actual transaction
   permissions instead of assuming every recently played player is droppable.
   [Yahoo roster and bench rules](https://help.yahoo.com/kb/SLN6869.html)
